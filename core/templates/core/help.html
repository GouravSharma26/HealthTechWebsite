Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an appointment? ▼
Simply navigate to the Doctors Directory from the main menu, find your preferred doctor based on their specialization or rating, and click the "Book Appointment" button. You will be able to choose from their available time slots.
How can I change my profile details? ▼
Log into your account, click on your name or profile picture in the top right corner, and select "Dashboard". From there, you can edit your personal details, contact information, and upload documents.
What happens if I miss my appointment? ▼
If you know you will miss your appointment, please use the "Request Reschedule" or "Cancel" feature in your dashboard as soon as possible. Repeatedly missing appointments without notice may result in your account being flagged.
Is my medical data secure? ▼
Yes. We use industry-standard encryption protocols to ensure that all your medical history, prescriptions, and personal details remain completely confidential and are only shared with the specific doctors you interact with.
